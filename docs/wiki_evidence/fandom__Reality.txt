Reality can be accessed once you unlock the first 13 rows of achievements and time dimensions 5-8, and have 1e4000 EP.

Reality is the 5th consecutive reset system and 3rd prestige layer, which resets everything apart from post-Reality achievements (i.e., those on rows 14+), statistics, Challenge/Infinity Challenge times, and everything after Reality.

##  What can I do now? 
Similar to Eternity, you can exceed 1e4000 EP whenever you want, and will receive more RM (Max: 1e1000, the cap can be increased by an Imaginary Upgrade) as a result of doing so.

On first Reality, the EP amount is softcapped past 1e6000 EP (Each 4 OoMs of EP past 1e6000 only count as 1 OoM of EP to the RM gain), and is hardcapped at 1e8000 EP (effectively 1e6500 EP)

RM 1-10 are linear, increasing every 104000/27x (3e4444 will give 4 RM). Beyond 10 RM, the formula becomes:

When your RM reached 1e1,000, you will produce Imaginary Machines, its cap is based on your highest EP (starts at 2.15e1,337,333 EP):

{(3(log(EP)/4000-1)-1000)}^{2}  \max({(3(log(EP)/4000-1)-100000)}^{0.2},1)

##  What next? 
On reality, you will gain 3 resources. Reality machines, which are based off the amount of EP you had when making a reality. Glyphs, has special effects that boost your production. Perk points, of which can be used to get upgrades to speed up gameplay. 

You will also begin to automatically unlock achievements from rows 1-13, at a rate of 1 every 30 minutes. You can pause this, which can be desirable if the auto-unlock is about to give you an easy achievement that you're about to unlock naturally, and you want it to give you a difficult/annoying achievement instead. This rate can be improved with Perks (specifically, those beginning with "ACH"), until the achievement reset is eliminated altogether by the ACHNR perk.
