Challenges are unlocked upon reaching Infinity. A challenge requires reaching Infinity Antimatter with special conditions, which change the gameplay. They will provide a unique Autobuyer upon completion. After the Reality Upgrade, you don't have to complete challenges to get the dimension and tickspeed autobuyers and instead need a certain amount of antimatter to unlock them. However, you need to complete challenges to be able to upgrade the interval.

You cannot go past 1.79e308 Antimatter inside a normal challenge, with a single exception.

##  Achievements 
Achievements related to Normal Challenges: (Note that these are mobile achievements)

* Daredevil: Complete 3 Normal Challenges.
* Antichallenged: Complete all the Normal Challenges (Reward: All Antimatter Dimensions are 10% stronger.)
* Zero Deaths: Get to Infinity without Dimension Boosts or Antimatter Galaxies while in a Normal Challenge. (Reward: Antimatter Dimensions 1-4 are 25% stronger.)
* You did this again just for the achievement right?: Complete the 3rd Antimatter Dimension Autobuyer challenge (C3) in 10 seconds or less. (Reward: 1st Antimatter Dimensions are 50% stronger.)
* Not-so-challenging: Get the sum of Normal Challenge times under 3 minutes. (Reward: All Antimatter Dimensions are stronger in the first 3 minutes of Infinities, but only in challenges.)
* Not a second lost: Get the sum of Normal Challenge time under 5 seconds. (Reward: All Antimatter Dimensions are 40% stronger, but only in challenges.)
* Many Deaths: Complete the 2nd Antimatter Dimension Autobuyer challenge (C2) in 3 minutes or less. (Reward: All Antimatter Dimensions are stronger in the first 3 minutes of Infinities.)
* Gift from the Gods: Complete the 8th Antimatter Dimension Autobuyer challenge (C8) in 3 minutes or less. (Reward: Dimensional Sacrifice is stronger.)
* This is fine.: Complete the Tickspeed Autobuyer challenge (C9) in 3 minutes or less. (Reward: Boost per buying 10 Antimatter Dimensions +1%)
