Lai'tela is the sixth Celestial, unlocked by purchasing the appropriate Imaginary Upgrade for 1e9 iM.

##  Dark Matter Dimensions 
Lai'tela gives a new currency called Dark Matter, which provides a multiplier to Continuum's effect based on the highest amount of Dark Matter you have ever had. Dark Matter is produced by Dark Matter Dimensions, in a similar cascading way to all other types of dimensions in the game. Unlike other dimensions, there are only four Dark Matter Dimensions rather than eight. You start with the first one unlocked immediately and the higher ones are unlocked via Imaginary Upgrades. When unlocking dimensions, you are given 1 of the dimension and cannot gain more without having it produced from the next tier up, or the Annihilation multiplier for the 4th Dark Matter Dimension after 5e11 Singularities.

Each Dark Matter Dimension, after a certain interval of time, generates two resources: Dark Matter or the next lower Dark Matter Dimension, and another resource called Dark Energy. Dark Matter and Dark Matter Dimension production per interval are equal to the product of your Dark Matter multiplier and the number of dimensions you have, while Dark Energy production is independent of your dimension amount. Dark Energy is used to produce Singularities, which have their own How To Play entry.

Your Dark Matter increases the number of purchases from Continuum. 

Dark Matter Dimensions can have their intervals upgraded down to a minimum of 10ms, at which point you can't upgrade the interval any further. You can choose to ascend Dark Matter Dimensions which reach that point, which initially multiplies Dark Matter gain by 500 and Dark Energy by 500. The interval gets multiplied by 1,200 but can be upgraded once again. Reaching 10ms again allows you to ascend again if you choose to.

##  Singularity 
Singularities are a new resource that you can obtain using features within Lai'tela.

To obtain Singularities, you need to reach 200 Dark Energy. When you do, you get the option to condense all your Dark Energy into a Singularity, resetting it back to zero. Any extra Dark Energy above this amount doesn't carry over and is thus wasted. Note that only Dark Energy is reset, the status of your Dark Matter and its dimensions stays the same when condensing Singularities.

Once you reach 10 Singularities, you can freely increase or decrease the Dark Energy requirement to condense Singularities by a factor of 10 (with a minimum of 200 Dark Energy). This increases or decreases the number of Singularities gained from resetting at the cap by more than a factor of 10 (determined by the Singularity Milestones of Increased Singularity gain per cap increase reached, the default factor is 11), making higher caps worth more if you are willing to wait.

The purpose of Singularities is to unlock Singularity Milestones, which act similarly to Eternity Milestones. Unlocking these milestones simply requires you to reach the total number of Singularities specified; Singularities are not spent. There are three types of milestones - one-time milestones, milestones repeatable a limited number of times, and milestones that can be repeated indefinitely.

##  Annihilation 
An Imaginary Upgrade allows you to unlock a prestige called Annihilation. Annihilation resets your Dark Matter and Dark Matter Dimensions but adds a permanent multiplier to Dark Matter that applies to all Dark Matter Dimensions. You can Annihilate multiple times; the additions to the multiplier stack additively, and there is no need to Annihilate for a greater addition each time. You must have at least 1e60 Dark Matter to Annihilate.

The Annihilation multiplier formula is:+log \biggr ( \frac 
{DM}
{e60} +1 
\biggr )^{1.5},multiplied by the Imaginary Upgrade 21 effect if you have this upgrade.

##  Lai'tela's Reality 
Lai'tela has a Reality that gives a multiplier to Dark Matter Dimensions' Production based on how well you do in the Reality. Whenever you complete the Reality in under 30 seconds, your highest available Dimension will be permanently disabled during further attempts of the Reality. Disabling all of your dimensions by completing the Reality in under 30 seconds eight times will also give you a ×8 multiplier to Dark Energy gain.

When you are in Lai'tela's Reality, Infinity Point and Eternity Point gain are Dilated, Game Speed is reduced to 1 and gradually comes back over 10 minutes, and Black Hole storing, discharging, pulsing, and inversion are all disabled. Antimatter generates entropy inside of this Reality.
