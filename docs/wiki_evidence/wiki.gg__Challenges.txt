Challenges are modified versions of reset layer runs that the player can enter at will. Completing the Challenges usually gives the player some kind of reward.


== Types of Challenges ==


=== Normal Challenges ===
The first set of Challenges the player sees requires the player to get 1.80e308 (Infinite) antimatter in a new Infinity with altered conditions. Challenges 1st through 9th are unlocked after the player performs at least one Infinity; challgnes 10th, 11th and 12th are only unlocked after 16 Infinities.
Completing the Normal Challenges allows the player to unlock and improve the basic autobuyers that the player gets at the start of Infinity layer.


=== Infinity Challenges ===
This set of challenges requires the player to get a certain amount of antimatter in a new Infinity with altered conditions.
Completing the Infinity challenges gives the player various rewards to different previous mechanics of the game.


=== Eternity Challenges ===
This set of challenges requires the player to get a certain amount of Infinity Points in a new Eternity with altered conditions. To be able to enter, the player has to have enough Time Theorems, as well as be eligible for a secondary requirement the challenge usually sets.
Completing the Eternity challenges gives the player various rewards to different previous mechanics of the game. The player can complete the Eternity Challenges repeatedly, up to 5 times, to make the reward from the Eternity Challenge stronger.
